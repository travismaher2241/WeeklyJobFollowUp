PLASGAIN JOB FOLLOW UP — PORTABLE EDITION

1. Copy "Plasgain Job Follow Up.html" to your work laptop.
2. Open it with Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome, already installed on the laptop.
   If double-clicking opens an editor, right-click > Open with > Microsoft Edge.
3. Choose your Ostendo Job Orders .xls or .xlsx export.
4. Check the preview and select "Prepare customer files".
5. Download one customer's Excel file, or all customer files in a ZIP.
6. Attach each customer's spreadsheet to your email.

Customers can reply "All dates confirmed", or enter confirmed or estimated dates and comments in
the yellow cells, save and email the spreadsheet back.

No installation, administrator rights, home-computer connection or internet
connection is needed. Your export is processed in the browser on your laptop.
Downloads go to your browser's normal Downloads folder.

Download the files before closing or refreshing the page. Closing the page
clears the loaded export. Your downloaded files remain saved.

Only the HTML file is required. This note is optional. Use your company's
approved method to transfer the file. The HTML contains no customer data.
